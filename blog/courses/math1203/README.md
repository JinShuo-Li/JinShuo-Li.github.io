Personal study notes for **MATH1203 Mathematical Analysis I** at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. They are my own review material and are **not official course materials**.

These notes focus on integration and series: antiderivatives and integration technique, the Riemann integral and its properties, improper integrals, and the theory of numerical series. The source archive is kept at [JinShuo-Li/Courses](https://github.com/JinShuo-Li/Courses).
