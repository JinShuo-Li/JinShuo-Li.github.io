Personal study notes for **MATH1204 Mathematical Analysis II**, the continuation of MATH1203 at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. They are my own notes and are **not official course materials**.

The course moves from series of functions and Euclidean-space topology into multivariable differentiation, multiple and line/surface integrals, field theory, parameter integrals, and Fourier series. A short set of ordinary-differential-equation notes is included as supplementary material. The source archive is kept at [JinShuo-Li/Courses](https://github.com/JinShuo-Li/Courses).
