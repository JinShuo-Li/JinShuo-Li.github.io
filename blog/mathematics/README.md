title: Mathematics
description: Notes on analysis, algebra, topology, and the structure that connects them.
---

Mathematics is the part of my work that changes how I think about everything else. These notes are about organization as much as content: definitions worth stating precisely, theorems worth proving carefully, and the connections that make a subject feel like one idea instead of many.

Most entries begin from a course or a book and end as a personal summary. They are not a substitute for a textbook, and they deliberately leave out material I do not yet understand well enough to write down.
